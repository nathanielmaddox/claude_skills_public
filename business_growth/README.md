# Business & Growth

Skills for growth strategy, sales, customer success, and financial analysis.

## Skills

| Skill | Command | Description |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| Growth Marketer | `growth-marketer` | Acquisition, activation, retention (Reforge + Hormozi frameworks) |
| Conversion Rate Optimizer | `conversion-rate-optimizer` | A/B testing, landing page optimization, funnel analysis, CTA optimization |
| Sales Expert | `sales-expert` | Irresistible offers, lead generation, conversions (Hormozi $100M frameworks) |
| Customer Success | `customer-success-expert` | Onboarding, retention, expansion, LTV (Gainsight frameworks) |
| Financial Analyst | `financial-analyst` | TCO analysis, ROI calculations, budget forecasting, vendor evaluation |

## When to Use

- **growth-marketer** — Growth strategy, acquisition channels, retention tactics
- **conversion-rate-optimizer** — Optimizing signups, checkout, or any conversion funnel
- **sales-expert** — Pricing strategy, offer creation, lead generation
- **customer-success-expert** — Onboarding flows, churn prevention, expansion revenue
- **financial-analyst** — Build vs buy decisions, ROI analysis, budget planning

## Usage Examples

```
Skill({ skill: 'conversion-rate-optimizer', args: 'Optimize the signup-to-paid funnel' })
Skill({ skill: 'sales-expert', args: 'Create pricing tiers for the ERP product' })
Skill({ skill: 'financial-analyst', args: 'TCO analysis: Convex vs Supabase' })
```
